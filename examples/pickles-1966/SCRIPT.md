# El perro que encontró la Copa (Pickles, 1966)

Vertical reel, 1080x1920, 30 fps, ~46 s. Spanish (Latin American) narration, es-MX-JorgeNeural. No captions.

## Narration
1. ¿Qué harías si tu perro encontrara, debajo de un arbusto, algo que medio país está buscando? *(hook, aimed at the viewer)*
2. Londres, marzo de 1966. Cuatro meses antes del Mundial, la Copa del Mundo se exhibe en una muestra de sellos… y alguien la roba de su vitrina.
3. Días después llega un paquete: un trozo de la copa y una carta pidiendo quince mil libras.
4. La policía detiene a un sospechoso… pero la copa no aparece por ningún lado.
5. Un momento. Una semana después del robo, un vecino del sur de Londres saca a pasear a su perro, Pickles.
6. Verás: Pickles olfatea un arbusto, y ahí, envuelta en papel de periódico, estaba la Copa del Mundo.
7. Inglaterra ganó ese Mundial. Y al banquete de los campeones… invitaron a un perro.

## Places (all enclosed: three rooms and a short night street)
- **The exhibition hall.** Oak panelling, tall windows, stamp cases and display boards, the trophy in a glass case behind a rope, and a guard dozing on his chair.
- **The chairman's office.** Bookshelves, a trophy cabinet, a desk with a green leather top, a banker's lamp, a telephone, the parcel with the piece of the cup, and the letter.
- **A south London street at night.** Brick semi-detached houses with lit windows, front gardens with privet hedges, street lamps, 1960s cars, and the newspaper bundle under the hedge.
- **The champions' banquet.** Red drapes, gold mouldings, chandeliers, the long table with the cup in the middle, and Pickles on a chair.

## Facts (checked)
- **The theft.** The Jules Rimet Trophy was stolen on 20 March 1966 from a stamp exhibition at Methodist Central Hall, Westminster, four months before the World Cup in England.
- **The ransom.** The FA chairman received a package containing the removable top lining of the trophy, with a note demanding £15,000.
- **The arrest.** A man was arrested during the police sting, but the trophy was not recovered.
- **The find.** On 27 March 1966 David Corbett was walking his dog Pickles in Upper Norwood, south London. Pickles sniffed out a bundle wrapped in newspaper under a hedge. It was the trophy.
- **The banquet.** England won the World Cup, and Pickles was invited to the winners' celebration banquet.
